# Marketing skills: sources and licence

The playbooks in `src/lib/skills/marketing.ts` are written for apsurn's in-app AI (Copilot, Writer, Operator).
They are condensed and adapted from the open-source **Marketing Skills** collection by Corey Haines
(https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills), specifically the `cold-email`, `copywriting`, `emails`,
`copy-editing`, `product-marketing` and `marketing-psychology` skills.
